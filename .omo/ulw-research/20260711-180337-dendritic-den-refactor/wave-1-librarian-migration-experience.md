## Digest
Reports are mixed: successful small and large migrations exist, but so do explicit choices of plain flake-parts over Den due to opacity/complexity. Reliable safety guidance converges on incremental coexistence and frequent checks, not a big-bang rewrite. Den adds a separate policy/graph debugging layer and has recent routing/context issues. No reliable average migration time or Den-specific rollback mechanism exists.
